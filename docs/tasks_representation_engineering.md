# Representation Engineering Tasks

This module contains metrics that read and manipulate high-level concepts in hidden states, following the top-down methodology of Representation Engineering (RepE).

**Registry coverage (4 tasks)**: `repe_concept_separability`,
`repe_refusal_direction`, `repe_steering_effectiveness`, and
`repe_task_vectors`.

**Paper-faithful vs. BLME adaptation notes**: `repe_concept_separability`
follows the released LAT concept-reading protocol
(`andyzoujm/representation-engineering`) and `repe_refusal_direction` follows
`andyrdt/refusal_direction`, on instruct models only. `repe_task_vectors`
computes the released ClusterMean reading vector but reports BLME's own
summaries of it. `repe_steering_effectiveness` is a BLME construct.

---

## 1. Task / Reading Vector Geometry
* **What are we measuring**: The geometry of contrastive activation-space directions ("reading vectors") across layers.
* **How are we measuring**: The RepE ClusterMean reading vector: the mean last-token state of true statements minus that of their false minimal pairs, at every entry of the raw hidden-state tuple. BLME then reports whether the per-pair contrasts d_i = h_i+ - h_i- agree (`pair_direction_cos_mean`, their mean pairwise cosine) and how large the consensus is (`consensus_energy_rel`, the mean over i != j of <d_i, d_j> divided by the mean squared residual norm, averaged over layers), plus the depth fraction of the largest-norm layer.
* **Hypothesis**: If a concept or behavior is linearly represented, contrastive hidden-state means should separate along a stable direction.
* **Citation/Paper**: `Zou, A., et al. (2023). Representation Engineering: A Top-Down Approach to AI Transparency.` [ArXiv: 2310.01405]. Related weight-space task-vector framing: `Ilharco, Ribeiro, Wortsman, Gururangan, Schmidt, Hajishirzi, & Farhadi (2023). Editing Models with Task Arithmetic.` [ICLR 2023, ArXiv: 2212.04089].
* **Paper summary**: Zou et al. propose Representation Engineering: instead of dissecting circuits bottom-up, read and control high-level concepts top-down from population-level hidden-state directions. Their LAT pipeline elicits a concept with contrastive stimuli and extracts a linear "reading vector" — §3.1.1 Step 3 lists the difference-of-cluster-means used here, taken at the last token of decoder models. They show honesty, emotion, and harmlessness concepts are linearly readable and controllable across open models. Ilharco et al. introduced the task-vector idea in *weight* space (fine-tuned minus pre-trained weights compose arithmetically); BLME measures the activation-space RepE analogue, not the weight-space one.
* **File & Function**: `src/blme/tasks/representation_engineering.py` -> `TaskVectorGeometryTask`
* **Critical Info**: Headline keys: `consensus_energy_rel`, `pair_direction_cos_mean`, `max_norm_layer_fraction`. The first two are declared invariant to a function-preserving residual rescale and to zero-padding of the width (`tests/tasks/invariance/test_repe_task_vectors.py::test_consensus_energy_invariant_under_residual_gauge_and_width_padding`). Raw vector norms move under those transformations and with the number of pairs. Defaults: `num_samples: 5`. These are not Hendel-style ICL task vectors or Todd-style function vectors, which need ICL task suites and causal head search. BLME reports measurement summaries only.

## 2. Concept Separability (LAT concept reading)
* **What are we measuring**: How linearly separable high-level behavioral or cognitive concepts (e.g., truthfulness vs deception) are in the representation space.
* **How are we measuring**: The released LAT concept-reading protocol for truthfulness: each true/false statement is wrapped in the LAT template and read at the last token (`rep_token=-1`) at hidden states -1..-(L-1). Per layer, the reading vector is PCA(1) of the training pairs' differences, with its sign set from the training labels; a held-out pair is correct when the true statement projects further along it. The layer is chosen on a validation split and `lat_test_acc` is the test accuracy at that layer.
* **Hypothesis**: LLMs represent high-level concepts via simple linear directions rather than complex non-linear circuits; a prerequisite for top-down control is that a linear probe separates the concept.
* **Citation/Paper**: `Zou, A., et al. (2023). Representation Engineering: A Top-Down Approach to AI Transparency.` [ArXiv: 2310.01405]
* **Paper summary**: See §1. Relevant here: the paper's "reading" experiments show concepts become linearly decodable in mid-to-late layers, with detection accuracy from linear readers (PCA-based LAT and logistic-regression directions) far above chance; BLME follows the released LAT reading protocol and reports the pairwise test accuracy at the layer chosen on a validation split.
* **File & Function**: `src/blme/tasks/representation_engineering.py` -> `ConceptSeparabilityTask`
* **Critical Info**: Headline keys: `lat_test_acc` and `lat_layer_frac` (the chosen layer's depth fraction), both declared invariant to a function-preserving residual rescale and to zero-padding of the width (`tests/tasks/invariance/test_repe_concept_separability.py::test_lat_headline_invariant_under_residual_gauge_and_width_padding`). A logistic-regression AUC on mean-pooled sentences is also reported as `_meta_`. Defaults: `num_samples: 20`; `lat_template: true`.

## 3. Steering Effectiveness
* **What are we measuring**: Whether adding a contrastive concept vector to the residual stream moves the output along the concept, more than a norm-matched random direction does.
* **How are we measuring**: The last-token mean difference of truthful/untruthful sentence pairs (`num_samples: 8`) is rescaled to a fixed fraction of the norm of the residual it is added to (0.1 at the default `steering_alpha: 1.0`), and added at the last position of a neutral prompt at each test layer. The effect is the signed change of next-token logits along the vector's own logit-space direction. Controls: the sign-reversed vector (-v), a norm-matched vector built from differences of the last-token states of unrelated sentences (Turner et al. 2023, App. G), and a data-defined token-anchor effect. All arithmetic is fp32.
* **Hypothesis**: If the concept is linearly represented, adding its vector should move the next-token logits along the concept's direction, reverse when the sign is flipped, and do so more than a norm-matched vector from unrelated sentences.
* **Citation/Paper**: `Zou, A., et al. (2023). Representation Engineering: A Top-Down Approach to AI Transparency.` [ArXiv: 2310.01405]; `Turner, A., Thiergart, L., Leech, G., Udell, D., Vazquez, J., Mini, U., & MacDiarmid, M. (2023). Steering Language Models With Activation Engineering.` [ArXiv: 2308.10248]
* **Paper summary**: Turner et al.'s ActAdd steers a frozen model by adding the activation difference of a contrastive prompt pair into the residual stream at inference — no optimization, no weight change — and shifts topic/sentiment while preserving fluency. Zou et al.'s control experiments use the same linear-combination operator over reading vectors (their `linear_comb` control with `token_pos="end"` corresponds to BLME's addition at the last position). The directional logit effect and its controls are BLME's own diagnostic.
* **File & Function**: `src/blme/tasks/representation_engineering.py` -> `SteeringEffectivenessTask`
* **Critical Info**: Headline keys: `steering_concept_over_random`, `steering_beats_random_frac`, `steering_semantic_effect`, `steering_semantic_success_rate`, `best_directional_effect`, `mean_directional_effect`, `best_directional_layer_frac`. `_meta_directional_consistency`, `_meta_directional_success_rate` and `_meta_steering_sign_reversal` summarise projections onto the steering direction itself and sit near 1 by construction (1.0 on pythia-160m with the per-layer directional effect negative at two layers), so they check that the estimand is directional rather than that steering works. The first two headline keys and those three are declared invariant to zero-padding of the width and to a function-preserving residual rescale (`tests/tasks/invariance/test_repe_steering_effectiveness.py::test_steering_keys_invariant_under_width_padding_and_residual_gauge`). KL(base‖steered) is sign-blind and is `_meta_` only. This is a BLME construct: ActAdd and CAA sweep a raw multiplier and score generations, which needs behaviour-specific classifiers or answer formats that base models do not follow. `steering_threshold` (default 0.01) sets the success rates.

## 4. Refusal Direction
* **What are we measuring**: Whether harmful and harmless prompts separate along a linear hidden-state direction.
* **How are we measuring**: Instruct models only (detected from the tokenizer's chat template, or forced with `is_instruct`). Each instruction is formatted with the release's template for the model family, without a system prompt, and prompts are kept only where the model's refusal score agrees with their label. Candidate directions are the difference of harmful and harmless means of the residual stream at the start of every block, at every post-instruction position. One direction is selected on validation prompts by the paper's rule: lowest refusal score after ablating it, subject to inducing refusal when added, KL < 0.1 on harmless prompts, and layer < 0.8 L.
* **Hypothesis**: Refusal behavior in aligned models is often mediated by a comparatively low-dimensional direction; stronger separation suggests a clearer refusal representation.
* **Citation/Paper**: `Arditi, A., Obeso, O., Syed, A., Paleka, D., Panickssery, N., Gurnee, W., & Nanda, N. (2024). Refusal in Language Models Is Mediated by a Single Direction.` [NeurIPS 2024, ArXiv: 2406.11717]. Related top-down framing: `Zou et al. (2023). Representation Engineering.`
* **Paper summary**: Arditi et al. show that across 13 open chat models, refusal is mediated by a *single* direction: the difference-of-means between mean activations on harmful vs harmless instructions (their §2.3: r = μ_harmful − ν_harmless). Ablating that one direction from the residual stream removes refusal (a white-box jailbreak), while adding it induces refusal on harmless prompts. BLME computes the same difference-of-means candidates and selects one with the paper's rule; it also reports the held-out separability of harmful and harmless prompts along the selected direction.
* **File & Function**: `src/blme/tasks/representation_engineering.py` -> `RefusalDirectionTask`
* **Critical Info**: Headline keys: `separability_auc`, `mean_projection_gap`, `direction_norm`, `rel_direction_energy`, `selected_layer_frac`, `refusal_score_bypass`, `refusal_score_induce`. `rel_direction_energy` is declared invariant to a function-preserving residual rescale and to zero-padding of the width; `separability_auc` and `selected_layer_frac` to the residual rescale only (`tests/tasks/invariance/test_repe_refusal_direction.py::test_rel_direction_energy_invariant_under_residual_gauge_and_width_padding`). Base models return NaN with a `_meta_` reason. The separability is a held-out 3-fold AUC of the projection on the train-fold direction. BLME reports the selected direction and the paper's selection scores.
